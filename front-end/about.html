About the Explorer

The Urban Mobility Explorer is a comprehensive visual analytics platform designed to analyze massive datasets of NYC Yellow Taxi trips.

Our platform focuses on identifying market inefficiencies and understanding fare dynamics across different New York boroughs. By leveraging high-performance data processing pipelines and modern web visualization technologies, we provide insights into travel patterns, pricing trends, and logistical outliers.

Key Features

  • Real-time Analytics: Instant computation of averages, standard deviations, and speed metrics.
  • Outlier Detection: Marking trips where passengers may be overpaying base on statistical distributions.
  • Spatial Mapping: Integrated geospatial views of taxi zones and trip distributions.
